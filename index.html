Cross-Platform Findings
Effort vs. Return Misalignment
Facebook received the most auditable effort (352 posts over 4 years) but generates the lowest measured engagement rate. Instagram has nearly 20% more followers with likely fewer Facebook-equivalent posts. Resources should be rebalanced toward Instagram, with Facebook maintained at a consistent but lower cadence.
Bilingual Audience Confirmed
Facebook post language data (58% Arabic, 40% English) and bilingual comment patterns confirm a mixed local-international audience. A structured dual-language content strategy — Arabic first, English follows — would serve both groups without doubling production effort.
Dormant Assets = Missed Reach
X/Twitter's 8,004 dormant followers represent a pre-built audience that has already opted in. Even 3 posts/week on X would reactivate the channel and extend reach at zero acquisition cost. This is the single easiest wins available to the communications team.
Quality Over Quantity — Confirmed
2022 had the highest volume (114 posts, 22.8/month) but not the highest average engagement (1.6). 2025 had fewer posts but the highest average engagement (2.2). More focused, high-quality posts outperform high-volume broadcast strategies on this account.

A consistent 3–4 posts/week (12–16/month) would improve algorithmic distribution without requiring more content — it requires redistribution of existing content production across the calendar. Build a 4-week rotating thematic calendar and assign ownership of each slot to a team member.

Establish a monthly analytics export routine: Instagram Insights export, Facebook post-level export, GA4 monthly report, LinkedIn analytics, and Patreon dashboard. T4P1 · 60 DaysAll Platforms

Pillar 02
Content Strategy: Unlocking the Archive
15 years of research, maps, and stories — waiting to become reach.

Grassroots Al-Quds holds one of the richest bodies of Jerusalem-focused content produced by any Palestinian civil society organisation. Village histories, neighbourhood maps, displacement documentation, heritage tours, and research reports represent an archive that — with strategic repurposing — can sustain years of high-performing social content. The data already shows that research-style posts average 2.2 engagement (29% above overall) and village infographics are among the top 10 performers. We build a system to unlock this at scale.

📚 Archive Audit & Content Library
We catalogue every research report, map, village profile, and heritage document in the Grassroots archive. Each asset is tagged by topic, format, platform fit, and repurposing potential — creating a searchable content library that the team can draw from instantly.
🗺️ Village & Neighbourhood Series
The Battir and Al-Walajah infographic posts are among the top 10 performers. We design a standardised "Village Spotlight" content format — bilingual, infographic-rich, cross-platform — that can produce 20+ posts from a single existing research document.
🎬 Short-Form Video Repurposing
Maps and infographics converted into 30–60 second Reels using animated formats. Historical photos turned into Instagram carousels. Research findings broken into Twitter/X threads. Each original document becomes 5–8 platform-native posts across Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn.
📅 12-Month Content Calendar
A fully populated 12-month content calendar built from the archive, with pillar rotation (Displacement, Research, Al-Aqsa, Heritage, Org Updates), optimal posting windows (Fri 4PM peak), and seasonal hooks tied to key dates in Palestinian history.
✍️ Bilingual Copywriting Framework
English posts outperform Arabic on Facebook (1.8 vs 1.6 avg). We develop a standardised bilingual post template — Arabic framing first for local resonance, English follows for international advocacy — with approved vocabulary guidelines for consistent voice across all platforms.
🏅 Prisoners & Detainees Content Sprint
Only 2 posts cover this theme — yet it achieves 6.0 average engagement (3.5× overall). We design a dedicated content format for deportation orders, detainee profiles, and legal updates, producing a 12-post monthly series that can be pre-loaded and scheduled.
